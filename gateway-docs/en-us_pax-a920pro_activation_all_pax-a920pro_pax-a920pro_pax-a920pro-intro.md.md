Introduction to the Acceptance Devices \| PAX A920 PRO Terminal {#pax-a920pro-intro}
====================================================================================

The PAX A920 PRO is a portable Android SmartPOS (point-of-sale) payment terminal that is suitable for retail and hospitality environments. These are the terminal's features:

* Device can be used with the [Acceptance Devices \| PAX All-in-One Solution](https://developer.example.com/docs/gateway/en-us/pax-all-in-one/integration/all/na/pax-all-in-one/pax-aio-intro.md "") and the [Acceptance Devices \| PAX Semi-Integrated Solution](https://developer.example.com/docs/gateway/en-us/sis-pax/integration/all/rest/sis-pax/semi-integrated-solution-pax-intro.md "").
* Internet connectivity options are a Wi-Fi network or a mobile network with a SIM card.
* Supported payment methods are contactless, chip with PIN, and magnetic stripe.
* Includes an integrated printer.

Introduction to the PAX A3700 Terminal {#home-merch}
====================================================

The PAX A3700 is a countertop Android SmartPOS (point-of-sale) payment terminal. It is designed for attended environments such as retail stores, hospitality venues, and service counters.  
The terminal has these features:

* Device can be used with the [Acceptance Devices \| PAX All-in-One Solution](https://developer.example.com/docs/gateway/en-us/pax-all-in-one/integration/all/na/pax-all-in-one/pax-aio-intro.md "") and the [Acceptance Devices \| PAX Acceptance Devices App](https://developer.example.com/docs/gateway/en-us/sis-pax/integration/all/rest/sis-pax/semi-integrated-solution-pax-intro.md "").
* Internet connectivity options include Wi-Fi or mobile network with a SIM card.
* Supports payment methods: contactless, chip with PIN, and magnetic stripe.

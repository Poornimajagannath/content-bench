Introduction to Paze {#home}
============================

Paze is an online checkout option, or *digital wallet*, that enables you to offer customers a fast and secure way to make purchases online. If you integrate Paze into your e-commerce page, you can process Paze transactions in the same manner as your standard card processing and you have access to the consumers enrolled in Paze.  
This guide describes how to submit transactions that originate from the Paze payment method to the `Payment Gateway` system for authorization.

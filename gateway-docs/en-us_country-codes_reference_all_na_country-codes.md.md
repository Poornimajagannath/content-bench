Country, Region, and Territory Postal System Reliability {#concept_smr_dhf_vpb}
===============================================================================

All postal systems are not created equally. The postal addresses of the world vary in their reliability according to how well a regional postal system can maintain its postal address database. DAV uses the databases maintained by these various postal systems to verify order address information. Countries, regions, and territories are grouped into five categories according to the reliability of their postal system databases.
If DAV cannot verify an address completely, or if the address cannot be matched with a postal database entry, you cannot receive a successful response. If the address is listed in a postal database, the service validates the accuracy of the address provided by the customer.

Category I-A {#reference_jzp_b3f_vpb}
=====================================

These countries or regions have rigorous testing and a postal certification process that results in the largest amount and greatest accuracy of postal data.

=====================================
